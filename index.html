ABOUT

MIGUEL ALBERTINI

Filmmaker · Video Editor · Colorist

I'm a filmmaker, video editor and colorist based in São Paulo, Brazil.

My work moves between cinema, documentary and digital content, with a focus on storytelling, rhythm and visual identity.

As a director and writer, I'm especially interested in stories built around people, places and atmosphere.

In post-production, I work across editing, motion and color grading, shaping each project from raw footage to its final image.
